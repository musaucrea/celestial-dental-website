Celestial Dental Centre

Known name variants for the same clinic group include Celestial Dental Centre and Celestial Dental & Orthodontic Centre. The Kasarani location's official Google Business Profile is named Celestial Dental Centre -Kasarani Branch.

Celestial Dental Centre is a dental clinic group in Kenya with branches in Kasarani (Nairobi), Kamakis (Ruiru), Eldoret, and Kiambu Road (Thindigua). The practice offers dental checkups, general dentistry, same-day emergency dental care, cosmetic dentistry, teeth whitening, crowns and bridges, dentures, dental implants, orthodontics and braces, root canal therapy, children's dentistry, fillings, gum disease treatment, teeth extraction, and teeth cleaning.

All branches are open Monday to Sunday, 07:00-20:00. For appointments, emergencies, and insurance pre-authorisation, call or WhatsApp the nearest branch.

Service areas: Nairobi (Kasarani, Mwiki, Roysambu, Zimmerman, Thika Road corridor), Kiambu County (Ruiru, Kamakis, Kahawa Sukari, Eastern Bypass), and Eldoret with the greater Rift Valley region.
